## Homeland and home (proposed)

Names Keldrath and Bramhold: owner-approved 2026-11-01. Everything else here stays proposed.

- **Keldrath** is the dwarven homeland, beneath highlands farther north than Elderreach and beyond the northern edge of the drawn map. It is a homeland name, not an added fifth known Region in Josh's list; political borders and its relation to the four Region divisions remain open.
- **Bramhold** is Toric's home, an inhabited underground Town in Keldrath. Workshops and shared kitchens open onto halls reached from surface trading entrances. It is one settlement, not an established capital or the only dwarven home.
- From Port Stocksmar, Toric describes home as north beyond Elderreach. Saint Nestroud remains in the mapped south-east. No bearing or shared border with Greefic Hill is assigned, and Toric's journey does not cross its uncrossable mountain. No coordinates, new map marker, playable route or tunnel to Lirold are proposed.

Source for these **(proposed)** facts: this foundation brief and the geography constraints above; used in `gear-upgrading-cold-forge.md` `aboutHome`.

## Everyday life (proposed)

- **Craft:** in Bramhold's shared workshops, a repairer puts a small tally beside a repaired joint so the next worker knows where to check. A sound repair earns respect alongside new work. These are ordinary workshop tallies, not an ancient script or a clue to the Stone Warden's marks.
- **Food:** mushroom-and-barley stew, dark bread and baked apples are familiar shared-kitchen meals. Surface traders bring grain and fruit; Bramhold is not sealed away from surface life. Fish & Apple Pie remains Toric's acquired coastal taste, not a dwarven staple or a new Recipe.
- **Surface folk:** Bramhold's workshop saying is that surface customers promise faster than metal cools. It is affectionate suspicion of hurried work, not hostility; a visitor who keeps a promise and cares for a tool is welcome at the table. Toric can speak for his own workshop, not every dwarf.

Source for these **(proposed)** customs: this foundation brief; used in `gear-upgrading-cold-forge.md` `aboutHomeLife` and [Toric's character](../character/lirold/toric.md).

## Why Toric left (proposed)

Before Slatefall's closure two winters ago, Toric left Bramhold voluntarily to keep a workshop of his own and learn what surface customers needed. Traders he met at home spoke of Lirold's repair work and regular Slatefall iron; that practical prospect brought him to Port Stocksmar's capital. He misses Bramhold's shared table, but the Forge is the life he chose. No exile, war or falling-out is implied; the year, travel itinerary and family remain unspecified.

Source: this **(proposed)** foundation brief, building on [The Cold Forge](../ideas/gear-upgrading-cold-forge.md)'s existing supply history; used there in `aboutLeaving`. Chronology: [history.md](history.md).
