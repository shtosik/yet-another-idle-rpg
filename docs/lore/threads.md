## The Cold Forge: resolved design and preserved threads

**(Proposed, `docs/ideas/gear-upgrading.md`, `docs/ideas/gear-upgrading-cold-forge.md`.)** Toric answers the gear proposal's unnamed-Blacksmith thread: his character and the Forge are recorded in factions.md/world.md, with the recovery timeline in history.md. This resolves the design brief, not a shipped mystery.

- T-17 stays open: Marvin checks a retained delivery record; he does not claim to have charted Slatefall or name his expedition's destination.
- T-21 stays open: no apprenticeship, family or former-employer link between Toric and Brenna is asserted.
- T-24 through T-27 stay open: Toric knows only that the castle closed the mine. He does not identify its ruler, the lost crew's fate, the stairs' makers or the cause of the blight. His dwarf identity is no evidence of dwarven ruins.
- Hedda keeps Oswin's badge; Pell keeps mine charting and Iron Ore. Toric recovers workshop tongs from the Mountain Pass and resumes salvage work, without reopening the mine or earning the Exploration Guild's writ.
- Step 4's shipped Fish & Apple Pie and the tongs contract are owner-settled: `ItemType.quest`, exactly 1 required, Goblin Scavengers at **1 in 50** only while `theColdForge` is active (`drop(ItemID.toricsForgeTongs, 1, 1, 50)`, last argument "1 in N"). Toric's taste for coastal cooking, XP/gold rewards remain Owner calls in the companion, not unresolved world mysteries. Pathfinder's Seal remains as approved.
- [Dwarven foundations](dwarves.md) fill the homeland gap with **(proposed)** Keldrath and Bramhold and a voluntary departure; T-29 keeps larger questions open. **Any link to the Slatefall stairs (T-25), the Stone Warden's marks (T-26), or the black sap and events of two winters ago (T-08/T-27) is undecided.** Workshop repair tallies are not a translation or identification of the Warden's marks. No connection to Messatan, the Hollow Door or the uncrossable mountain is established.

Answered: T-07 (which Region holds Mawood) is Elderreach (then Southback), an owner decision recorded in world.md. T-22 (Southback lying north) and T-23 ("North" Lirold lying south) were closed by the shipped renames to Elderreach and Lirold (owner decision, 2026-10-27).
