It pays for Guild charts and returns them signed with a seal, never a name (Varrow, T-24). **(mine, bounties, charts: proposed, `docs/ideas/slatefall-and-the-deeper-mines.md`, `north-lirold-and-exploration-guild.md`)** | owner decision |

Sylvie wrote to them for years, and her word is why one stranger who fights outside the gate is let in. **Wick**, a shy drover's boy, keeps the tack oiled for a herd lost in the sap winter. Marta burned their orchard herself, because the black came up through the grafts, and her table went from thirty to six; what happened to the herd is T-31. The Guild asks for monster-carried wild seed. Names are placeholders. | `farmWarden.json`, `farmDrover.json`, `mawoodSylvie.json` `questTerrace`; `docs/ideas/farm-dialogue-scripts.md` |
