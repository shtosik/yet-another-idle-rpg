# Town building upgrades & reputation

**Scope:** M · **Status:** Proposed

Spend gold + materials to upgrade buildings in La Harpar and Mawood: better shop stock, shorter refresh cooldown, new dialogue branches. Big gold / material sink. A kitchen or alchemist upgrade could unlock [consumable](consumables-buff-slots.md) recipes.

## Touch-points
`towns.store.ts`, `towns-data.ts`, `shop.store.ts`, dialogue data.
