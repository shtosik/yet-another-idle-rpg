# High Platform (`TownBuildingID.mawoodHighPlatform`)

## Purpose
[Milo's](../mawood/milo.md) perch — highest point in Mawood, near the canopy. Quest hub for Feathers for a Kite / What Lurks at the Top.

## Atmosphere
Breaks from the rest of town's twilight-canopy gloom — this is the one place that gets real sky. Should feel exposed, windy, a little precarious: makeshift rope rigging, salvaged kite parts, a kid's-eye-view lookout rather than an adult dwelling. Contrast against the Elder Tree/Apothecary/Lodge is the point.

## Background art brief
Small platform lashed high on a Spire, thin rope bridges visible leading off-frame, open sky and canopy leaves rather than deep-forest twilight. Scavenged furnishings — driftwood-style found materials, a half-built kite, feathers and a spyglass. Brighter, cooler light than the rest of Mawood.
