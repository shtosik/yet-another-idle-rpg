# Apothecary (`TownBuildingID.mawoodApothecary`)

## Purpose
[Sylvie's](../mawood/sylvie.md) shop and home. Sells healing items/reagents, gives the Silk and Water / Infestation quest line.

## Atmosphere
Per design doc, explicitly "carved into a Spire trunk" — organic hollowed space, not a built wooden shed. Warm counterpoint to the rest of Mawood's twilight: drying herb bundles, hanging vials, spider-silk skeins, a cluttered-but-loved workspace. Should feel lived-in and busy, matching Sylvie's talkative energy.

## Background art brief
Curved bark walls (interior of a hollowed trunk), shelves built into the wood grain rather than freestanding furniture. Herbs/dried flowers hanging to dry, glass vials catching moss-light, mortar and pestle on a root-shelf counter. Soft green/lavender palette.
