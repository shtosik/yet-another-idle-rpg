# Hunter Lodge (`TownBuildingID.mawoodHunterLodge`)

## Purpose
Shared building for [Finn](../mawood/finn.md) (hunter/trapper, sells arrows/traps) and [Brenna](../mawood/brenna.md) (carver/armorer). Only Mawood building housing two NPCs — Finn positioned left (x:35), Brenna right (x:65).

## Atmosphere
Built wooden lodge rather than grown-into-trunk like the Elder Tree/Apothecary — a workshop, not a dwelling carved by nature. Should visually split into two halves matching each NPC's trade: Finn's side rougher, hides/pelts/snares/arrows hung on the wall; Brenna's side more ordered, pale calcified Silverwood bark planks stacked and half-carved armor pieces on a bench.

## Background art brief
Single interior, two work zones. Left: trapper gear, animal pelts, a fireplace or drying rack. Right: carpentry bench, wood shavings, bark strips in varying stages of cure, carving tools. Warm lantern light against the twilight-canopy exterior glimpsed through a window/doorway.
