# Elder Tree — Town Center (`TownBuildingID.main`)

## Purpose
Mawood's central platform, home to [Corwin](../mawood/corwin.md). Quest hub for the whole "Roots of Rot" corruption questline.

## Atmosphere
The oldest, largest Silverwood Spire in town — the trunk itself is the building, hollowed and grown around rather than built onto. Twilight canopy light, bioluminescent moss glow in blues/pale greens. Should feel ceremonial and ancient next to the more workaday shops — carved root-columns, hanging lanterns of glowing fungus, maybe a faint rune-scar pattern in the bark echoing Corwin's own corruption-marked skin.

## Background art brief
Interior or platform-edge view carved into a giant trunk. Wide gnarled roots as furniture/structure. Pale silver-grey bark walls, moss-lit rather than sunlit. Should read as the tallest/oldest structure in the skyline if visible from outside.
