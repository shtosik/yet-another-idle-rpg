# Magic Shop (`TownBuildingID.mawoodMagicShop`)

## Purpose
[Elarion's](../mawood/elarion.md) shop — runes, spellbook, magic reagents. Added later than the original design doc; not part of the original five-NPC roster.

## Atmosphere
Corruption gave the local heartwood arcane conductivity ("corrupted heartwood conducts arcane energy like copper conducts heat" — Elarion's own words), so this shop should visually lean into that: glowing root-veins running through floor/walls, crystallized rune stones on shelves, a chaotic ex-court-mage's clutter rather than Sylvie's tidy-ish apothecary. Glowing warded door per his intro line.

## Background art brief
Small cluttered shop, root-veins glowing faint arcane color through the wood grain (distinct hue from the moss-light elsewhere in town — pick something Elarion-specific, e.g. violet/cyan vs. the town's ambient green). Shelves of vials and rune stones, a scorch mark or two (his "occasionally set things on fire" line), a spellbook on display.
