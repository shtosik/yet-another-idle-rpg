# Elara — Traveler, Seeking Her Father

## Personality
Defensive-turned-earnest half-elf, used to stares ("Can you stop staring? Yes, those are my ears."). Driven by a single goal — find her missing father, Captain Theron Tidecaller, a treasure seeker gone five years. Fiercely loyal to him even while admitting his flaws ("best at what he does, but terribly naive"). Sharp tongue toward the villagers she's stuck waiting among ("Useless. All they do is hang out in that stinky tavern.") but warms fast to anyone actually helpful.
