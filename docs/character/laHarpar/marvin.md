# Marvin — Exploration Guild member (La Harpar)

## Personality
A veteran who teaches, direct and competent, and the one villager Elara and the Trader both single out. La Harpar-born, home a few days from an expedition he won't talk about, not sleeping well, and in no hurry to go out again. He trains anyone who asks and takes it seriously. Encouraging without flattery ("Fine work out there!"). He has one unlikely love: the Guild's road book, which he keeps and carries, and which holds every cart that ever passed a Guild post (`Phurgo#coldForgeProgressThree`: "He'll want to tell you about the book. Let him.").

## Voice sheet
Tendencies, not laws: the habits below come and go.

- **Sentence length:** medium and plain, with short imperatives when he drills ("Count."). Runs long and happy on the book, and on shinies.
- **Stories:** gives the end first, then what it cost, usually about a recruit by name. No build-up, no audience.
- **Funny:** seldom and without trying. A joke is an aside on the way to the lesson. He isn't La Harpar's wit; Josh and the Bartender are.
- **The player:** a recruit he's responsible for. Assumes they'll get it wrong, is pleased when they don't, and tells them which.
- **Asks about:** what you'd do, what you got wrong.
- **Grammar habit nobody else in La Harpar has:** teaches through a supposition: "Say you need a sack of crab shell." He sets the situation and makes you stand in it.
- **Game terms:** none. He doesn't talk in systems. Numbers appear the way a trainer uses them (count to three, one piece in ten). He says stretch of road rather than tier, far end rather than Wave. Names of knacks (Haste, Weaknesses, Shiny Hunter) are hunters' words to him.
- **Moods:** curt and kind with Elara's question. Talks about the Guildmaster with respect and a little wariness. Warm about Phurgo, whom he manages with a pie.

## Tics
1. "Say you..."
2. Lessons nobody asked for, offered when a question gets close.
3. "Right." when someone gets something right, and telling them whether they were good or guessed.

**Will not say plainly:** where he went, what he saw there, and that he doesn't want to go back. Near it he hands you a drill. Whether he saw anything of Theron is left open (T-33).
