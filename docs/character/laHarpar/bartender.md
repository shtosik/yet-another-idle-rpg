# Bartender — Tavern Owner

## Personality
Perpetually irritated, monetizes everything, including basic conversation ("Ask away, though my answers aren't free" / charges 20 coins just to state the obvious). Holds grudges over small slights ("I'll remember your face, smartass"). Runs a one-woman operation buried in unpaid work (crab meat shortage from "lazy rats" loitering) but never actually turns business away. Dismissive of Josh's tab-mooching, respects Marvin's competence.
