# Josh — Tavern Fisherman

## Personality
Lazy, transactional charm — literally sells his own attention for beer ("Pay for my next beer and I'll talk to you"). Self-aware deadpan about being a lech ("I'm a local fisherman, and my hobby is drinking free beer"). Turns surprisingly informative once paid — knows regional geography, trade, and the forbidden mountain's dark rumors in real detail. Undercuts his own menace with a quick backpedal ("I got carried away").
