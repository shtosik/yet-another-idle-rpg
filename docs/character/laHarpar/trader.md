# Trader — General Goods Shopkeep

## Personality
Loud, self-promoting salesman humor, in on his own joke ("finest shop in all of La Harpar" — the only shop in town). Genuinely helpful town-info hub once past the pitch — knows everyone (Josh, the Bartender, Marvin) and directs players accordingly. Proud of the village's fishing reputation, treats it as personal accomplishment. Casual dockside speech pattern ("aye").
