# Hedda Brask — Tavern Keeper

**(proposed, `docs/ideas/slatefall-and-the-deeper-mines.md`)**

## Personality
Dry, practical, few words. Pours strong and talks less. Widow of Oswin Brask, the mine foreman lost with his crew two winters ago; she keeps the grief behind the bar and turns sympathy aside ("That'll do."). Wants the mine reopened because the village dies without it, and wants to know what happened to Oswin. She doesn't ask for pity; she asks for jobs done.

## Voice
- Short declaratives, often fragments ("My husband. Foreman.").
- Speaks for the village ("We roof the capital and the capital forgets us").
- Never says Oswin is dead until the end; "he got that far", "he's with his crew".
- No jokes, but a dry edge ("either brave or lost").

## Avatar concept
Woman in her late forties, broad-shouldered, grey-streaked dark hair tied back under a scarf. Sleeves rolled, leather apron over a wool dress, a bar cloth over one shoulder. A brass ring on a cord at her neck. Tired, steady eyes. Lamp-lit amber against slate grey.

## Setting
The Last Lamp: low stone taproom, slate floor, a big lamp hung over the door, miners' helmets on pegs that nobody has come back for.
