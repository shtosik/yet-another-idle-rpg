# Old Tobin — Retired Miner

**(proposed, `docs/ideas/slatefall-and-the-deeper-mines.md`)**

## Personality
Half-deaf, loud, stubborn. Forty years down the mine; came up the week before the collapse, "the week the stone sang", and nobody believed him. Grieves his crew (Bram, Nell, the Corrie twins, young Dace, with Oswin) by naming them. Wants the stairs seen so he isn't "deaf old Tobin" any more, and his crew at rest.

## Voice
- "Eh?" to open; "Speak up!"; "Sit where I can hear you."
- Shouts one word per line in capitals (SANG, STAIRS, FORTY) and repeats the key word.
- Plain miner's words: levels, the bottom, digging; never "galleries" or fancy terms.
- Keeper of the hint (T-27): he notices the timing, never claims to know the cause.

## Avatar concept
Old man, seventies, bald with a white fringe and big bushy eyebrows, a hand cupped behind one ear. Coal-grimed creases, a miner's leather cap, a pipe. Thick forearms gone thin. Squinting, half-cheerful, half-haunted.

## Setting
A corner seat in the Last Lamp, close to the fire, a tankard and an old miner's lamp on the table.
