# Pell — Guild Apprentice Mapmaker

**(proposed, `docs/ideas/slatefall-and-the-deeper-mines.md`)**

## Personality
Nervous, precise, earnest. Two years at the Guild's map table in Lirold, apprenticed to Guildmaster Odile Varrow (`docs/character/lirold/varrow.md`); sent to chart the Slatefall mine for the castle's decision to reopen it. Has never been underground and doesn't want to be. Quietly in awe of Varrow. Wants a finished chart and her nod.

## Voice
- Starts sentences twice ("You're — you must be…"), "um", apologises for asking.
- Quotes the Guildmaster ("The Guildmaster says a chart nobody walked is just a story").
- Exact numbers (ten samples, fourteen levels).
- Small humour aimed at himself or Varrow ("practically a parade").

## Avatar concept
Young man, about twenty, thin, ink-stained fingers, round spectacles, sandy hair. Guild-orange armband over a grey coat, a satchel stuffed with rolled charts, a drawing board under one arm, a pencil behind the ear. Looks over his shoulder at the mine mouth.

## Setting
The Last Lamp, a table covered in half-drawn mine charts weighted down with ore samples.
