# Warden Marta Tillbrook (`farmWarden`) — Farming Guild head

Source: `docs/ideas/farm-and-husbandry.md` §3 (names are placeholders).

## Personality
Blunt, sunburnt, counts in seasons. Closed the Farming Guild's gate after the black sap and does not apologise for it. Warms slowly: cold to a stranger, dry-funny once the player has earned a seat (the "rats steal cheese" joke). Respects work, not bravery. Has not cooked for anyone in two years.

## Speech habits
Blunt and dry, in natural full sentences with connecting words and varied length, never telegraphic fragments. Calls the player "walker" now and then. Plain farm metaphors, no flourishes: *"Dirt doesn't care how brave you are."* Never gushes; her praise is a nod and a better offer. Gives times in minutes and hours, not "soon".

## Voice sheet
Tendencies, not laws: she strays from them with mood and topic.

- Middling, complete sentences, the reason hanging off "because" or "and". Orders in the future tense ("you'll").
- Answers exactly what was asked and stops, so the player has to ask again. Rarely volunteers an ending.
- Funny seldom and dry, more often once the player has worked for her.
- Stranger: a hired hand on trial, no jokes. Worker: useful, first dry remark. Trusted: asks the player's opinion. At the table: fewer defences, not more words.
- A clean crop makes her breathless, almost girlish. Tiredness shortens her. Near a hard question she turns it round and questions the player.
- Will not say plainly: who knocked at the gate that winter.

## Backstory (owner-approved 2026-11-07, shipped in `farmWarden.json`)
Came up the valley at seventeen to pick for one harvest, thirty-six harvests ago, and told the old Warden over supper that his wheat had gone soft; he gave her the keys for it when his hands went. Thirty ate at her table before the sap, six now. She burned the orchard herself because the black came up through the grafts. **Never stated:** drover Perrin brought the herd back that winter, they knocked, and she left them outside because beasts carry seed, with Wick shut in the seed store so he wouldn't hear. Her line stays "the sap took them". See T-31.

## Portrait brief
Weathered woman in her fifties, strong forearms, a sun-bleached straw hat pushed back over grey-streaked brown hair, a deep tan with sharp crow's feet. Patched green work coat over a rolled-sleeve linen shirt, a ring of small brass keys at the belt. Mood: stern and watchful, a trace of tired kindness at the mouth. Terrace greens and earth browns, warm afternoon light.
