# Wick (`farmDrover`) — the Farming Guild's drover

Source: `docs/ideas/farm-and-husbandry.md` §3 (names are placeholders).

## Personality
Fifteen, shy, a tanner's boy who labels everything. Brave only when the subject is animals. The Guild's herds were lost in the sap winter, so he keeps the tack oiled and the stalls swept anyway. Hopeful in a quiet way; husbandry (farm doc §11) is his later arc.

## Speech habits
Speaks in full, shy sentences, with an occasional "um" or trailing "...", not in every line. Starts a sentence about people, finishes it about animals. Lists what he needs by purpose ("for the, um, saddle blankets"). Never calls the player by a title; says "you" and then looks at his boots.

## Portrait brief
Lanky teenage boy with a mop of straw-coloured hair, freckles, big wary eyes and a half-shy smile. Oversized leather apron stained with dye, sleeves too long, twine and paper labels pinned to the front, a coil of rope over one shoulder. Mood: nervous but warm, easily startled. Soft browns and tan leather against terrace green.
