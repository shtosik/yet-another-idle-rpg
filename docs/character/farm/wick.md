# Wick (`farmDrover`) — the Farming Guild's drover

Source: `docs/ideas/farm-and-husbandry.md` §3 (names are placeholders).

## Personality
Fifteen, shy, a tanner's boy who labels everything. Brave only when the subject is animals. The Guild's herds were lost in the sap winter, so he keeps the tack oiled and the stalls swept anyway. Hopeful in a quiet way; husbandry (farm doc §11) is his later arc.

## Speech habits
Speaks in full, shy sentences, with an occasional "um" or trailing "...", not in every line. Starts a sentence about people, finishes it about animals. Lists what he needs by purpose ("for the, um, saddle blankets"). Never calls the player by a title; says "you" and then looks at his boots.

## Voice sheet
Tendencies, not laws: he strays from them with mood and topic.

- Full, shy sentences that begin about people and drift to animals. Sometimes an "um" or a sentence that gives up.
- Gets lost in stories, goes back, corrects a small detail nobody asked about.
- Never funny on purpose. Asks what the player saw out there and whether it looked well.
- Tends to speak of the lost herd in the present tense (Bramble, the grey one, thirty-one head).
- A badly handled hide or a sick animal makes him quick, firm and almost bossy, with no apology in him.
- Stranger: sorry you've had to speak to him. Regular: expects you back. Trusted: stops apologising and tells you things without noticing what they are. Friend: shows you the calf names.
- Will not say plainly: that the herd is gone. Near it he goes to an animal and stays there.

## Backstory (owner-approved 2026-11-07, shipped in `farmDrover.json`)
His tanner father walked him up to the Guild after a buyer laughed at the notes Wick wrote on hides ("Somebody was kind to this one"). Bramble stood on his foot the first morning. In the sap winter he spent three days and nights in the seed store rewriting labels on Marta's order, hearing the gate's "bad hinge". He has no idea what that means. See T-31.
