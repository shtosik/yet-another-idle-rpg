# Odile Varrow — Guildmaster of the Exploration Guild

**(proposed, `docs/ideas/north-lirold-and-exploration-guild.md` §4, §5, §8b)** Town: Lirold. `NpcID.liroldVarrow`. Meeting her sets `met_liroldVarrow`; from then on she, not Marvin, handles the player's contracts and the Guild store (stage 1).

## Personality
Head of the Exploration Guild, in its only hall. Ex-cartographer in her fifties who walked half the charts on her own wall before she took the ledgers. Serious, exact, and hungry for what other people have seen. She loves blank space on a map and will forget her manners over a sightline. She keeps track of everyone through traders and members' letters and trusts each fact exactly as far as its source. She sends people where she wants charts (Marvin's last expedition, Pell to Slatefall), and she writes down who hasn't come back. In letters she is terse (Pell quotes them, `docs/ideas/slatefall-and-the-deeper-mines.md`); across a table she talks more than she knows.

## Voice sheet
Tendencies, not laws: the habits below come and go.

- **Sentence length:** medium to long, complete and well made, in no hurry. She goes short when she's busy, when a subject is closed, or when she's frightened.
- **Stories:** told as routes: where it was, which way, how far, who says so. People come in late. She rarely knows she's telling a story.
- **Funny:** never. She doesn't joke and doesn't notice when something she says is funny. Ludo Crane owns Lirold's deadpan anecdote; she must not end scenes on a dry closer.
- **The player:** a source first. She assumes they noticed things and took bearings, and is put out when they didn't. "Hunter" until *The Guild Trial* is done, then nothing. After the trial she asks rather than assigns.
- **Asks about:** where, which way, how far, who told you.
- **Grammar habit nobody else in Lirold has:** she grades her facts inside the sentence by where they came from ("I have that from the Trader's carters too, so I believe it"; "I've only his word"). "In pencil" means unconfirmed and "in ink" means she'd stake the Guild on it; use each rarely.
- **Moods:** the castle's clerks make her curt. A blank on the map makes her run on. A hint about Milo's door rattles her into plain short sentences.

## Tics
1. Opens on a bearing or a question about ground before any greeting.
2. Grades her sources aloud.
3. "Enter it", "on my roll", "open": her ledger words, in the plain sense.

**Will not say plainly:** how many members she has sent out who never reported, and that she keeps their lines open. Near it she asks for a bearing and repeats the question until you answer it.

|---|---|---|---|
| Sentences | Long, complete, measured | Big, bellowed, breaks for the stands | Short, subject dropped |
| Stories | Routes and sources | Performances with a pause and a guess | Avoids them, except pie |

## Facts she knows
Marvin's letters, Mawood traders, the Trader's carters and Elara's seasonal letters (proposed, `la-harpar-quests.md` T4). She doesn't know why Slatefall shut, what the castle hides or who reads her charts (T-24). She knows where Marvin went and won't say (T-17).

## Avatar concept
Tall, lean woman in her fifties, iron-grey hair in a tight braid, sharp grey eyes, ink-stained fingers. Dark slate-blue long coat with a Guild-orange sash, a brass compass on a chain, reading spectacles pushed up on her head. Stands at a map table under a wall of charts.

## Setting
The Exploration Guild hall in the capital: high stone room, a big map table, shelves of ledgers, Guild-orange banners, a training yard through a door, a window with a view of two gates, a wall of charts with pins that stop.
