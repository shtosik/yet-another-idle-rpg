# Odile Varrow — Guildmaster of the Exploration Guild

**(proposed, `docs/ideas/north-lirold-and-exploration-guild.md` §4, §5, §8b)** Town: Lirold. `NpcID.liroldVarrow`. Meeting her sets `met_liroldVarrow`; from then on she, not Marvin, handles the player's contracts and the Guild store (stage 1).

## Personality
Head of the Exploration Guild, in its only hall. Ex-cartographer in her fifties who walked half the charts on her own wall before she took the ledgers. Precise and measured, dry rather than warm. Keeps track of everyone through traders and members' letters, so she knows the player's deeds before they speak. Sends people where she wants charts (Marvin's last expedition, Pell to Slatefall) and rarely says why. Trust is a ledger entry: earned, recorded, never assumed.

## Voice
- Calls the player "hunter".
- Ledger and chart words: "the ledgers keep more than contracts", "on no chart of ours".
- Short, complete sentences; no exclamation marks. Dry humour by understatement ("He sends everyone; few arrive.").
- Answers questions about the Guild's secrets with a condition ("Raise your rank and ask me again").
- Quotes Marvin's letters, never gossips herself.

## Avatar concept
Tall, lean woman in her fifties, iron-grey hair in a tight braid, sharp grey eyes, ink-stained fingers. Dark slate-blue long coat with a Guild-orange sash, a brass compass on a chain, reading spectacles pushed up on her head. Stands at a map table under a wall of charts.

## Setting
The Exploration Guild hall in the capital: high stone room, a big map table, shelves of ledgers, Guild-orange banners, a training yard through a door.
