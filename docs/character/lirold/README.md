# Lirold — characters

Lirold is the capital of Port Stocksmar (`laHarparJosh.json` `beachName`). Its shipped Buildings are the Arena, with Ludo Crane (`dialogues/liroldArenaMaster.json`), and the Forge, with Phurgo (`dialogues/liroldPhurgo.json`); the Exploration Guild hall below is a proposal.

- [Ludo Crane](ludo-crane.md) — shipped Master of the Arena (`src/assets/locales/en/dialogues/liroldArenaMaster.json`); character doc retains its original proposal label
- [Phurgo](phurgo.md) — dwarf Blacksmith at the Forge, loves apple pie and acquired a taste for La Harpar's Fish & Apple Pie (`docs/ideas/gear-upgrading.md`) — portrait done (first pass)
- [Odile Varrow](varrow.md) — proposed Guildmaster at the Exploration Guild hall (`docs/ideas/north-lirold-and-exploration-guild.md`) — avatar to do

Shared look: stone and slate city, castle at its centre. Palette: limestone white, slate blue-grey, Guild orange (the Guild), crimson and gold (the Arena).
