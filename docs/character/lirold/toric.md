# Toric — Blacksmith

**Proposed, [Gear upgrading](../../ideas/gear-upgrading.md), owner brief 2026-11-01.** Town: Lirold. New Building: **Forge**. Proposed NPC key: `liroldToric`; display name **Toric**. A dwarf who loves apple pie is owner-approved; the details below are proposals until shipped.

## Personality

Toric keeps bent nails sorted by size and tools clean even when there is no work for them. Deliberate, exact and quietly generous, he hates waste more than he hates being kept waiting. He will straighten a stranger's loose buckle while insisting he has not agreed to take a job.

Slatefall's closure cut off his regular iron deliveries two winters ago. He has done cold repairs since, keeping the hearth ready for a shipment that never arrives; losing his heavy tongs to scavengers gave him another reason to postpone starting again. His reluctance is partly pride: he wants his old work back and dislikes admitting that salvage could be worthwhile work of its own.

Apple pie breaks through that reserve. He asks for a proper meal before lighting the hearth, makes room at the workbench for a plate, and listens while he eats. The player is helping him begin again, not paying a joke toll. Coastal traders taught him to enjoy La Harpar's Fish & Apple Pie as a hearty workshop lunch; this is his personal taste, not a custom assigned to all dwarves. The Quest uses that shipped dish.

He knows Slatefall through deliveries and workshop business, not through its buried mysteries. Any link between dwarves and the mine's stairs, the Stone Warden's marks or the black sap is undecided (T-25–T-27); Toric offers no special knowledge of them.

## Home and departure (proposed)

Source: [Dwarven foundations](../../lore/dwarves.md), **(proposed)** for his home conversations in [The Cold Forge](../../ideas/gear-upgrading-cold-forge.md). Toric comes from **Bramhold**, an underground Town in **Keldrath**, the dwarven homeland beyond the drawn map north of Elderreach. His age, family and exact birthplace remain open.

He learned his habits in shared workshops, where a repair tally beside a joint tells the next worker where to look. He misses suppers of mushroom-and-barley stew, dark bread and baked apples at the shared table. His workshop's joke that surface customers promise faster than metal cools conceals warmth: someone who keeps a promise gets a chair. Fish & Apple Pie is still his later coastal discovery.

He left voluntarily to run his own workshop and learn unfamiliar work. Surface traders spoke of Lirold's repairs and Slatefall iron; he came before the closure two winters ago and chose the Forge as his home. There is no exile or homeland disaster behind his reserve. He answers questions about home plainly, without becoming a spokesman for all dwarves or introducing their wider history.

## Voice

- Short, concrete statements: heat, grip, weight, seams, useful metal.
- Measures before promising; praise is precise: "Jaws still meet. Good."
- Dry warmth rather than shouting, unlike Ludo Crane; no phonetic accent, stock mining catchphrases or drinking jokes.
- Talks about apple pie with unguarded care: "Let the apples soften. Don't hurry them."
- Explains upgrades honestly: two matching pieces become one stronger piece, with gold for his work. He does not claim to restore the mine or conjure iron.
- Avoid calling the player a pathfinder before the Seal is registered; usually addresses them without a title.

Sample: "A blade can have life left in it after its owner gives up. People, too, if you give them a chair."

## Portrait brief

Broad, compact dwarf with large working hands, a square face and a dark beard streaked with iron-grey, tied back clear of the hearth. Calm brown eyes under heavy brows; a small, almost reluctant smile. A smoke-dark leather apron over a rolled-sleeve slate-blue shirt, copper apron rivets, an old burn scar on one forearm. No royal regalia or warrior armour.

Waist-up, readable at NPC portrait size, one hand resting on an anvil and the other holding a small pie plate away from the soot. Hammer and long tongs in the background rather than crossing his face. Pale stone, slate and warm copper connect him to Lirold; a soft ember glow suggests the restored Forge. The pie is a humanising detail, not a giant comic prop. Portrait remains to be generated; no art created in this pass.
