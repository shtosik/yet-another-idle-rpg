# Ludo Crane — Master of the Arena

**(proposed, `docs/ideas/lirold-arena-master.md`)** Town: Lirold, Building: Arena (`TownBuildingID.liroldArena`). `NpcID.liroldArenaMaster`, display name "Ludo Crane". Replaces the placeholder name "Varek".

## Personality
The Arena's master, announcer and gatekeeper. Eleven seasons its champion and never beaten on the sand; a cart wheel on market day took his knee, not a fighter, and the castle gave him the gate. Loud, warm, a showman who never stops playing to the stands, even when they are empty. Honest about money: the fee is the fee, and he says so up front. Proud of his champions and fond of Yad the way a keeper is fond of a beast that could kill him. Knows the city's gossip, so he knows the player killed the harpy queen before they arrive.

The opposite of Guildmaster Varrow (`varrow.md`): where she is dry and counts in ledgers, he booms and counts in sand.

## Voice
- Calls the player "challenger".
- Opens big: "Ha!", exclamation marks, talks as if the crowd can hear.
- "Sand" for everything: the Arena floor and the sandglass ("the sand's still falling", "beat the sand").
- Hints, never instructions: says what a champion does, not which button to press ("the one that shakes has teeth in it").
- Deflates his own legend with a joke (the cart wheel).

## Voice sheet
Tendencies, not laws: the habits above come and go.

- Big and warm by default: talks past the player to the stands and describes them in the third person ("this one can count"). Performs stories, holds the pause, makes the player guess.
- The only teller of deadpan anecdotes in Lirold, usually at his own expense.
- Money, supplies and the knee bring him to ordinary volume, like any tradesman. So can an empty building and a real question.
- Will not say plainly: what it is to stand at the gate with a stick while someone else goes down. Near it he gets louder and reaches for a better story.

## Backstory (owner-approved 2026-11-07, shipped in `liroldArenaMaster.json`)
Raked the Arena floor at ten for a copper a day. The cart was on market day, outside the chandler's, a bag of onions in his hand. The player can bring him 10 Spider Silk for the knee (Mawood people wrap joints in it); this is a dialogue favour with no Quest and no reward. Champions: the Brute's river-ward pits and midwinter ham, the Arcanist reading her contract aloud, the Burrower's turnip bill. Yad is a monster and only that: it bites poles and won't eat while watched.

## Avatar concept
Broad, heavy-set man in his fifties, thick neck, shaved head, a big grey moustache and a booming open-mouthed grin. Old pit scars on his forearms. A crimson-and-gold showman's sash over a sleeveless leather jerkin, a battered bronze champion's buckle on his belt. Leans on a cane cut from an old spear shaft, one knee bandaged. One arm raised as if calling to the stands.

## Setting
The Arena gate in Lirold: a raked sand floor, stone stands filling with a crowd, a huge sandglass on a post by the gate, crimson banners.
