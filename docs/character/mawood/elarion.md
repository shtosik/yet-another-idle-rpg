# Elarion — Magic Shopkeep

## Personality
Eccentric ex-court mage, chatty, self-deprecating (jokes about accidental fires). Scholarly delight in corruption's arcane side effects despite the danger. Scattered but sharp — wrote up the whole rune-crafting process himself.

## Avatar concept
Slim androgynous mage, singed sleeve cuff, mismatched court-robe-meets-shopkeep-apron. Cracked monocle or floating rune shard near hand. Soot smudge on cheek, wild expressive brows, mischievous grin. Vial shelves behind.
