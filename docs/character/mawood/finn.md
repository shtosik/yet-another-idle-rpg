# Finn — Trapper / Scout

## Personality
Terse, guarded, distrustful of strangers ("You armed? Good"). Lost his leg to a treant 8 years ago, walks it off, no self-pity ("My own fault. I know that."). Withholds respect until earned, then loyal ("I'll remember that").

## Avatar concept
Weathered ranger, short practical hair, fur-lined trapper vest, scar through eyebrow. Carved oak peg-leg visible. Bow/snare slung on back. Hard eyes, faint softening at the corner. Green-brown forest tones.

## Setting
Design doc: "Finn Ashwood," sells arrows/traps, shares the Hunter Lodge building with Brenna. Background should read hides/pelts/snares/arrows — his half of the lodge — against Brenna's bark-carving half.
