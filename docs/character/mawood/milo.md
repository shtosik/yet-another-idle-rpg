# Milo — Orphan Kid, Canopy Scout

## Personality
~8-10 years old, reckless, fast-talking, overconfident bravado hiding real competence (tracks harpy patrols Finn dismisses). Craves being taken seriously ("Probably. But you've handled worse.").

## Avatar concept
Small kid, wind-tousled hair, oversized hand-me-down jacket, rope/climbing harness. Freckles, wide grin, feather tied to belt. Lean-forward posture like mid-climb. Bright eager eyes.

## Setting
Design doc pitched him as "Street Kid/Pickpocket" originally — dialogue text moved him toward harpy-watcher/scout instead, no pickpocketing shown. Go with the dialogue framing for the portrait (spyglass/kite parts, not a thief's kit). Lives on Mawood's highest platform, brightest/windiest point in town — background should feel more open-sky than the twilight-canopy tone of the lower buildings.
