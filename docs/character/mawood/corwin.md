# Corwin — Town Elder

## Personality
Slow, weighty, patient speech. Ancient guilt — botched a ritual decades ago trying to commune with a Silverwood Spire, bark fused into him instead. Protective of Mawood, self-blame over the 2-year corruption spread ("If I knew the source, I would have stopped it years ago"). Closes conversations with dry wit ("Or don't rest — that also seems to be your nature").

## Avatar concept
Old man, pale silverwood bark grown up one side of neck/jaw into hairline, patch fused over collarbone/shoulder. Deep-lined weathered face, calm heavy-lidded eyes. Simple elder wrap/robe, faint pale-green rune glow where bark meets skin. Staff of bone-white spire-wood.

Design doc (`docs/design/mawood-and-elderwood-zones.md`) calls him "Elder Corwin," half-blind. Worth folding in: one eye clouded/milky, faint unfocused gaze — reads as "sees the forest more than the room." Origin per dialogue is a specific failed communion ritual decades ago (not a generic curse) — bark is the ritual's aftermath, not an injury.

## Setting
Lives at the Elder Tree building (`main` tab), Mawood's central platform. Twilight-lit by bioluminescent moss/fungi, not sunlight — art should use cool pale-green ambient glow rather than warm daylight.
