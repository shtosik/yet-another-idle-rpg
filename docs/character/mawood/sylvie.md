# Sylvie — Apothecary

## Personality
Warm, talkative, nurturing, self-aware of her own chatter ("I know I talk a lot"). Homesick for La Harpar, worried for her friend Elara. Anxious undertone about the corruption but scientifically engaged (antidote research).

## Avatar concept
Gentle-featured woman, hair half-braided with dried herbs/flowers, apron with vial pouches. Freckled, rosy warm expression. Holding a small vial or mortar. Soft green/lavender palette against apothecary shelves.

## Setting
Design doc confirms: apothecary "carved into a Spire trunk." Background should show the room as bored/hollowed into living bark (curved organic walls), not a built wooden shed like the Hunter Lodge.
