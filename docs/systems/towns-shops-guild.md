# Towns, shops and the Exploration Guild

## Purpose
Towns are non-combat hubs. Each town has **buildings**; a building holds NPCs (dialogue) or is a zone entrance. Shops and the Exploration Guild are reached through NPC dialogue.

## Key files
- `src/app/services/forge-selection.ts:ForgeSelection` — the Forge session shared by the inventory, equipment and Forge panels: both picked sources (refusing one copy in both, `sharesOneCopy`), `quote` (cost, chance, +1 gear, also while refused), `evaluation`, and `merge` (uses up the sacrifice; an upgraded inventory base stays selected as its new stack).
- `src/app/components/game/town/forge/forge.component.ts:ForgeComponent` — inline gear merge panel: labels, stat preview and result toast.
- `src/data/towns-data.ts:TOWNS_DATA` — per town: `regionId`, background `url`, `buildings[]` (`buildingId`, `position` of the button on the town view, `npcs` with positions, `url` background, optional `zoneId` and `questRequirement`).
- `src/data/sites-data.ts:SITES_DATA`, `src/enums/ids/site-id.enum.ts:SiteID` — a **Site** is a non-combat place that is not a Town (the farm); it has a map marker and an entry in the map tab's Sites list (beside Towns), unlocked through `unlockedContent.sites` (the `unlockSite` dialogue effect). A Site has `npcs` (positioned in %, like a building's) and a `gate` button position. Its home screen is `components/game/site/` (`SiteComponent`, `GameTab.farm`, the map's 2 x 2 footprint with equipment and quests on the right): background, `app-town-building` rendering the NPC frames (its input is just `npcs`, `url` and an optional building id), dialogue overlay, inline shop and the Gate button, which opens the plots panel. It needs no selected Town: NPC frames and dialogue read only the NPC id, so the Site never touches `selectedTownId`. `GameNavigationService.siteId`/`openSite` pick the Site; the map's `siteSelected` calls it. `NpcPlace` is `{ kind: 'town', townId, buildingId } | { kind: 'site', siteId }` (`npcPlace` searches towns, then Sites; `isNpcPlaceUnlocked` checks the town or Site) and `talkTo(place)` opens either; the quest list's "Talk to" link uses both. The farm has two NPCs, Warden Marta (`farmWarden`) and Wick (`farmDrover`). `validate` accepts a quest's `turnInNpc` standing on a Site.
- `src/enums/map/town-id.enum.ts:TownID`, `town-building-id.enum.ts:TownBuildingID`, `npc-id.enum.ts:NpcID`, `region-id.enum.ts:RegionID`.
- `src/interfaces/town.interface.ts:Town`, `TownBuilding`.
- `src/app/store/towns/towns.store.ts:TownsStore` — `selectedTownId`, `selectedTownBuildingId` (computed `selectedTownBuilding` from `TOWNS_DATA`), `activeShopId` (not saved).
- `src/app/components/game/town/towns.component.ts` — town view, building buttons, shop/building switch; `town-building/`, `npc-frame/`, `shop/`.
- `src/data/shops-data.ts:SHOPS_DATA`, `src/types/shop/shop.type.ts:Shop`/`ShopItem` — static stock: `itemId`, `tier`, `price`, `maxStock`, `refreshable`, `minRank`, `minReputation` (`{ reputationId, tier }`); per shop `currency` (gold or `explorerTokens`), `buys` (item kinds it buys) and `sellMultipliers`.
- `src/app/store/shop/shop-prices.ts:sellPrice` — what a shop pays for an item.
- `src/app/store/shop/shop.store.ts:ShopStore` — runtime stock per shop and refresh timer (`ensureShop`, `tickCooldowns`, `buyItem`); `src/types/shop/shop-state.type.ts`.
- `src/app/services/exploration-guild.service.ts:ExplorationGuildService` — task generation; task state lives in `PlayerStore` (`activeTasks`, `explorerTokens`, `totalTasksCompleted`).

## Forge panel
The dialogue effect `{ type: 'forge' }` calls `TownsStore.openForge()` and swaps the building view for `ForgeComponent` inline, like a shop. Opening a shop closes the Forge and opening the Forge closes the shop. Closing the panel returns to the building; changing building, town or leaving the town tab closes it. `forgeOpen` is transient and never saved, like `activeShopId`. Phurgo in Lirold's Forge opens it after The Cold Forge is completed; every Upgrade gear dialogue result checks completion. `await dev.openForge()` bypasses the quest for testing over La Harpar's first building.

The desktop panel has three 144px slots: two inputs on the left and an output preview on the right, with gold cost (navbar coin; red when the player can't afford it), success chance and the Upgrade button between them. Empty slots show no placeholder icon. Like a town building background, a painted workshop backdrop fills the whole Forge panel (`src/assets/img/ui/forge-workbench.png`, compressed to 960×536 PNG), with a dark wash and vignette to keep its labels and captions above 4.5:1 contrast; the workbench row has no frame of its own. The title matches the shop/dialogue's gold 1.4rem Cinzel heading. Slots have inset bronze frames that retain rarity glows and mismatch/milestone markers; the central iron plaque uses the shared panel shadows and button mixin, with a muted, readable disabled Upgrade button and a fixed width so chance and cost appearing never shift the slots. A decorative 48×48 pixel-art anvil (`src/assets/img/icons/forge-anvil.png`) sits on the plaque above chance/cost at native 1× scale with `image-rendering: pixelated`, empty alt text and `aria-hidden`. The first input is the piece to upgrade (base), and the second is the copy to consume (sacrifice), with short captions beneath each. The output appears only for two matching, upgradable copies; incomplete pairs, missing copies, mismatches, capped, unique and unsupported items have no upgraded preview. Gold and capacity refusals retain the preview. Non-pet equipment icons are native 48×48 pixel art, displayed at 96×96 (2× with `image-rendering: pixelated`). The player's gold balance stays in the navbar.

While `TownsStore.forgeOpen` is true, inventory switches to Gear. The other tabs are visibly disabled, ignore click and keyboard navigation, and explain the lock in their tooltip. Closing the Forge, opening a shop or leaving the building/town unlocks them. Inventory and equipment slots accept mouse click, Enter/Space or native HTML5 dragging as Forge selection, rather than equipping/unequipping. Drag sources use `ForgeDrag` on their existing buttons; `ForgeSelection` holds typed, transient source references across these sibling panels. Each Forge input accepts drops into that specific slot; external drags are ignored. No drop-list containers or new dependency are needed. Clicking or activating a filled input with Enter/Space empties it; activating an empty input focuses the first Gear item as a keyboard selection shortcut. Inventory destruction controls are hidden while selecting for the Forge.

Click, Enter/Space and assistive activation fill the first empty input; when both are filled, they replace the second input. Clicking one inventory stack twice selects it for both inputs only while the stack holds two copies; with one copy the second input stays empty. Selecting an equipped slot already used by the other input is ignored, including targeted drops. There is no automatic pairing or preference for equipped gear: the two selected sources are passed directly to `evaluateMerge` and `PlayerStore.mergeItem`. Both inputs must have the same id, rarity and upgrade level; a mismatch marks both inputs red and appears in the Upgrade button's accessible label and the output tooltip. An equipped base stays equipped; an equipped sacrifice is consumed and its equipment slot empties. Only the output slot has a tooltip; it shows only the item, its stats (including before/after bonuses from `gearModifiers`, fractional stats and the +5 milestone when a preview exists), and any refusal reason. Phurgo explains the rules in his dialogue (`forgeWork`, `forgeCost`, `forgeLimits`). Player-facing text says Upgrade; code keeps the merge API names.

`evaluateMerge` supplies refusal details (missing copy, unique gear, cap, gold or inventory capacity including its tab). The compact Upgrade button stays focusable with `aria-disabled`, its accessible label contains the refusal, and its handler guards activation. Tooltips also open on keyboard focus and dismiss on Escape or blur. `PlayerStore.mergeItem` rechecks and applies the merge. A three-second live status toast reports success or a later-level failure; only gold and the sacrificed copy are lost on failure. After success, the first input follows the upgraded inventory stack or keeps its equipped slot; after failure, the first input stays unchanged. Either outcome empties the second input. A refusal leaves both inputs untouched. Closing the panel clears both inputs and unfinished drags. All text uses the `forge` English namespace.

## Data flow
**Navigation**: the world map's town marker calls `TownsStore.selectTown` and switches to the town tab. Clicking a building either enters its zone (`zoneId` → `BattleStore.setZone`, back to battle) or opens the building view with its NPC frames. A building with `questRequirement` is hidden until that quest has started. Leaving the tab clears the selection.

**Town view**: the town name sits top left, followed by the player's reputation tier in towns that keep reputation; building buttons highlight on hover and focus. `GameComponent` preloads every unlocked town's background and building backgrounds, so they don't pop in on first visit.

**Reputation** (`src/app/store/player/reputation.ts`, design in `docs/ideas/town-reputation.md`): `PlayerStore.reputation` saves points per `ReputationID` (laHarpar 1, lirold 2, mawood 3, farmingGuild 4: the old `TownID` numbers, so old saves load unchanged); they are never spent or lost. Only `REPUTATION_IDS` (La Harpar, Mawood, Farming Guild) keep it; the Reputation tab shows the Guild once the farm is unlocked; `REPUTATION_TOWN` maps one to its town. The tier (`ReputationTier`: Neutral 0, Friendly 50, Honored 200, Revered 600, Exalted 1500, cumulative `REPUTATION_THRESHOLDS`) is derived, never saved. Quest rewards give reputation (`quests.md`), and so do **turn-ins**: `src/data/reputation-turn-ins.ts:REPUTATION_TURN_INS` lists four givers, each taking one bundle of one drop per turn-in at a flat rate (one bundle every 2 h, see `dialogue.md`), for reputation only (Bartender 20 Crab Meat and Josh 20 Feather for 10 La Harpar; Sylvie 15 Spider Silk for 15 and Brenna, `mawoodBrenn`, 10 Ancient Wood for 25 Mawood). Items the current steps of active quests need are kept back (the option says how many), and the options disappear once the town is Exalted. Each giver's hub node (Josh's is `talkReady`, after the beer or his Friendly `talkFree`) has one option, "Hand over 20 Crab Meat (have 34)", tagged with the reputation it pays; without a full bundle it shows one bundle's worth, disabled, and while that bundle cools down it carries a "Ready in 1 h 12 m" tag. `validate` checks every giver stands in the town they credit and uses their turn-in. `npm run sim -- --reputation` prints turn-in reputation per hour and the hours to each tier. Every gain goes through `ReputationService.gain`, which calls `PlayerStore.addReputation` and shows a tier-up toast with one line per town that went up, however many tiers it crossed; `GameComponent` renders it above dialogs. The quest panel's Reputation tab lists each unlocked reputation town with its tier and a bar to the next threshold (full at Exalted), with no reward lists. Guild rank is separate: tasks never give reputation. Cheat: `dev.addReputation(reputationId, amount)`.

**Tier rewards** (design in `docs/ideas/town-reputation-tier-rewards.md`): `PlayerStore.reputationTierOf(reputationId)` is what everything checks.
- **Conditions**: dialogue conditions and quest requirements take `{ type: 'reputation', reputationId, tier }` (`dialogue.md`, `quests.md`); a shown lock reads "Requires Mawood Honored — 140/200".
- **Friendly**: the Bartender answers his three paid questions for free (his place question gets its own `whatIsThisPlaceFree` line) and Josh talks without the beer (`talkFree`, once; it sets `josh_paid_beer`). Sylvie opens the apothecary to a Mawood Friendly player before *Silk and Water*.
- **Honored**: shop rows with `minReputation`. Trader: Cheese, Venison Roast Recipe, Wharf Runner's Boots (3000). Apothecary: Minor Mana Potion, Greater Mana Potion Recipe. Elarion's magic shop: Rootwalker Boots (6000).
- **Revered**: one hidden option each, no reward: the Bartender's "The usual, please." (`onTheHouse`), Josh's heirloom (`aboutHeirloom`, after *Clearing Out the Beach*), Brenna's commissions (`reveredCommissions`, after *Raw Materials*) and Corwin's first shoot (`reveredFirstShoot`, after *The Blighted Heart*).
- **Revered quests** (`QUEST_DATA` 16–19, gated by the Revered tier): *Safe Passage* (Trader: Troll ×3 and 40 Goblin Scavengers, then tell Sylvie; +100 La Harpar and +25 Mawood, the first cross-town reward), *Last Orders* (Bartender: 10 Venison Roast and 10 Fish & Apple Pie), *A Clean Cure* (Sylvie, after *The Infestation*: 10 Elder Silk and 5 Spider Fang, then 30 Corrupted Saplings) and *Roots and Rafters* (Brenna, after *War Trophies* and *The Blighted Heart*: 30 Ancient Wood and 20 Spider Silk, then Corwin blesses it and leads into `reveredFirstShoot`). Each goes first in its giver's quest hub; the two talk steps have `requirements: []` and their own option on the turn-in NPC's `default` node.
- **Exalted pets** (design `docs/ideas/town-reputation-pets.md`): the Bartender gives the Tavern Cat (`default.tavernCat`) and Corwin the Rootglow Moth (`default.rootglowMoth`), each once: the option needs the town Exalted and its flag (`bartender_gave_pet`, `corwin_gave_pet`) unset, and the gift result sets it. A full Pets tab disables the option (`missingSlots`) without setting the flag. See `enemies-shiny-pets-bestiary.md`.
- **Farm shop** (`ShopID.farmWardenShop`, Warden Marta, `farmWarden`; opened from her dialogue by "Let me see your seeds." once `theFirstFurrow` is started (the first time through `shopFirst`, flag `marta_shop_explained`), and by her no-seeds intro): Turnip Seeds 20 g always (stock 20), Wheat Seeds 60 g from Farming Guild Friendly (stock 10), Harvest Pie Recipe 400 g at Honored, Mushroom Stew Recipe 600 g at Revered. She buys nothing. Sylvie's apothecary also sells the Glowcap Elixir Recipe (400 g).
- **Titles** (`src/data/titles-data.ts:TITLES_DATA`, `TitleID`): Friendly and Exalted in each town earn one (Friend of / Toast of La Harpar, Friend of / Keeper of Mawood), plus **Hand of the Terraces** at Farming Guild Exalted only (`TitleID.farmingGuildExalted`, source `ReputationID.farmingGuild`). `PlayerStore.earnedTitles` is derived from tiers, never saved; `selectedTitle` is saved and `selectTitle` accepts only an earned title or `null`. Display only, no stats; the picker is on the stats panel.

**Dialogue**: clicking an NPC portrait starts their dialogue, shown as an overlay over the building inside the town panel, so the inventory below stays visible during turn-ins. Leaving the building (or opening a shop) closes it.

**Shops**: a dialogue `shop` effect calls `TownsStore.openShop(shopId, stage)` and records the speaking NPC in the saved `npcShops` (and the stage in `npcShopStages`); from then on that NPC's frame shows a **Shop** button that opens the shop directly at that stage; the town view swaps to `ShopComponent` inline (not a modal). **Stock stages**: the effect's optional `stage` (default 0) hides items whose `minStage` is higher. Stages share one runtime stock, so two NPCs can open the same shop at different stages and an unbought unique item stays in stock at the next stage. No shop uses stages yet. `ensureShop` creates runtime stock on first visit and re-syncs (by item id) when the static list changes. Buying checks stock, Guild rank (`minRank`), town reputation (`minReputation`), the shop's currency, station order for station gear, then inventory space; it pays (`PlayerStore.spend`, gold or tokens by the shop's currency; it refuses and changes nothing when the balance is short), decrements stock, then grants the item (an atomic Item grant, `PlayerStore.grant`: station gear installs, the Spellbook unlocks the spellbook tab instead; `blockReason` asks `canGrant`, so neither needs a slot). This lives in `ShopStore`: `blockReason(shopId, index)` and `purchase(shopId, index, count)`, which returns `{ bought, spent, installed }` or the block reason when nothing was bought. Shift-click buys up to 5 (`purchase` with `count` 5), stopping at the first block, with one currency float for the total; `ShopComponent` only shows labels, the float and the station-installed modal for `installed`. Both tabs are a grid of inventory-sized slots: stock (or stack size) in the slot, price with a coin or token icon under it, name and details in the item tooltip. Buy block reasons show on the slot: red border = can't afford, lock with `R<rank>`, a tier name or `Lv <level>` = Guild rank, town reputation or station level missing, orange border = the item's inventory tab is full (the label and hover text name the tab), faded = out of stock; each slot's `aria-label` names the reason. A token shop's header shows the Guild rank; the token balance is in the HUD strip. The **Sell** tab lists the inventory stacks this shop buys at `sellPrice`: `value × multiplier` rounded up, then multiplied by `2 ** upgradeLevel` (missing level means +0; item multiplier wins over type; a green price marks "Pays more", above ×1); `buys` limits the kinds, and `buys: []` hides the tab. Click sells one, Ctrl/Cmd-click the whole stack (`PlayerStore.sellItem`). Preferences: the Trader pays ×1.5 for sea catch and ×1.25 for food; Sylvie buys only resources, potions and food, ×1.5 potions and reagents; Elarion buys only resources and gear, ×1.5 runes and rare parts. Every second `tickCooldowns` counts down; at 0, refreshable items reset to `maxStock` and the timer restarts at the player's `shopRefreshCooldown`.

**Lirold**: the Arena, with Ludo Crane, who takes the fee and starts Combat Arena runs (`arena.md`), and the Forge (`TownBuildingID.liroldForge`, 14), with Phurgo (`NpcID.liroldPhurgo`, 12). Both buildings are accessible once Lirold opens. Phurgo offers The Cold Forge and conversation before completion, then Upgrade gear. Art: Forge background `backgrounds/liroldForge.png`, Phurgo avatar `avatars/lirold/liroldPhurgo.png`, tongs icon `items/phurgosForgeTongs.png`. Quest details: `quests.md`; character brief: `docs/character/lirold/phurgo.md`.

**Exploration Guild** (Marvin, La Harpar): dialogue `guild` effects/conditions drive it. **Guild rank** = `floor(totalTasksCompleted / 5)`, no cap. `acceptTask(length)` rolls a target uniformly from the regular enemies of zones that are unlocked and have `tier <= rank + 1`, and a count (short 25–50, medium 50–200, long 200–400). Reward = 1/2/3 tokens × the target zone's `tier`. Up to `maxTaskSlots` tasks at once (stat, base 1). A kill progresses every matching task, by 2 with `taskDoubleKillChance` percent (Bounty Mark). `claimTask` claims every finished task; `abandonTask` (`taskIndex`) cancels that unfinished task; Marvin lists one cancel option per task, naming its monster and kills left. Clicking a finished task in the battle nav bar opens Marvin's dialogue in the guild building (`GameNavigationService.talkToMarvin`, which also owns the open game tab). Conditions: `taskComplete` (any finished), `hasActiveTask` (any unfinished), `noActiveTask`, `hasFreeTaskSlot`, `taskInProgressAt` (`taskIndex`).

**Guild store** (`explorersGuildStore`, from Marvin's hub): priced in Explorer Tokens, buys nothing. Rank 0: mana potions, recipe scrolls, Skill Point Book; rank 1: Guild Satchel (+2 slots to each of the Gear, Consumables and Materials tabs, 1 in stock), Hunter's Feast recipe, Ambush Scroll (25 tokens, one); rank 2: Second Contract (+1 `maxTaskSlots`) and Bounty Mark (+10% `taskDoubleKillChance`), one each.

**Pathfinder's Seal registration**: Marvin's hub places "Register this Pathfinder's Seal." after The Cold Forge's talk turn-in and before ordinary Exploration Guild business. It appears only while holding a Seal with no permanent `upgradedDropChance` bonus. The atomic `registerPathfindersSeal` effect takes exactly one Seal and saves the permanent 10% chance for eligible gear drops to arrive at +1; the saved bonus also prevents repeat registration and further Seal rolls. A registered player instead sees "What did the Pathfinder's Seal teach me?" and its permanent-lesson reminder. Registration needs neither The Cold Forge nor a Guild rank. The scripts follow `docs/ideas/gear-upgrading-cold-forge.md`; Ludo's optional Seal lore points to Marvin in La Harpar.

## Intended behavior (owner decisions)
- Gold spending is blocked when the player can't afford it (shops already check).
- Two tasks may roll the same monster; one kill then progresses both. Intended: a lucky roll.

## Invariants & gotchas
- `TownBuildingID` keys name the building's i18n label (`map:buildings.<key>`) and its button; a zone entrance has no `url`.
- Runtime shop items are aligned with `SHOPS_DATA[shop].items` by index after `ensureShop`; `buyItem` and refresh use the index.
- The selected town and building id are saved; `activeShopId` isn't, so a reload never reopens a shop. Selecting a town or building (including leaving the town tab, which clears both) closes the shop; selecting a town also clears the building.

## Known issues
- The town picker hard-codes La Harpar; other towns are reached only from the world map.
- Guild store upgrades use placeholder icons (silk, letter, gnoll trophy).
- Gold shop prices weren't re-tuned after the zone rescale: late-zone income makes the 5000-gold items cheap.
- The shop template calls `buyBlockReason(i)` per item per change detection; buy logic lives in the component.

## How to extend
New building: the `add-town-building` skill. New shop: the `add-shop` skill. New guild task type: extend `GuildEffect`/`GuildCondition` (see `dialogue.md`) and `ExplorationGuildService`.
