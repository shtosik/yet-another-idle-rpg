# Anatomically correct arthropod sprites (crabs, spiders, scorpions, insects)

Date: 2026-10-17. Scope: why our SDXL pipeline draws crabs wrong while Gemini gets them right from a bare prompt, and how to reach that quality on the local RTX 4070 Super (12 GB VRAM, ~26 GB free disk). The sections below are the research; the measured outcome is first.

## Outcome (2026-10-18, run on our machine)

- **E1, real-reference ControlNet: works for layout, but line art copies the source's interior edges.** Line art from a Gemini crab and from a Z-Image crab gave correct anatomy; the plate-like double edges made shells look metallic. A filled silhouette fixes only the outline and leaves colour and markings to the prompt.
- **E2, Z-Image-Turbo as structure source (12.3 GB bf16 + 5.6 GB encoder + 0.34 GB VAE, about 9 s per image): crabs yes, spiders no.** Crabs came out with two claws, stalk eyes and legs on both sides on all 8 seeds; faint faces on the shell appeared on about 3 of 8 until a source without one was picked. All 16 spiders (3/4 front and top view, with "exactly eight legs") had the wrong leg count. The count claim in section 1 holds for distinct parts, not for many identical thin limbs.
- **What shipped:** `--zimage` (structure candidates), `--source` (SDXL img2img repaint of a frozen source, denoise 0.45), and a frozen spider silhouette control. Crabs: Z-Image source plus repaint. Spiders: silhouette control of one hand-checked Z-Image spider. See `docs/analysis/asset-pipeline.md` § Anatomy and controls and § Species recipes.
- **Not run:** FLUX.2 klein (E4), the Gemini API (E3), Z-Image ControlNet. The Gemini image the user supplied was used only as a line-art source.

## TL;DR

The anatomy problem lives in the **text encoder and model scale**, not in our style stack. SDXL works from two CLIP encoders. CLIP behaves like a bag of words, and SDXL's counting score is far below models conditioned on T5 or an LLM. Gemini, Qwen-Image, Z-Image and FLUX.2 [klein] all condition on a large language model. Two models since mid-2025 change the options for us: **Z-Image-Turbo** (6B, Apache-2.0) and **FLUX.2 [klein] 4B** (Apache-2.0). Both fit 12 GB and our disk, and they share one text encoder. Use one of them, or Gemini, to produce **structure**. Keep SDXL + IP-Adapter + LayerDiffuse + post-process for **style and alpha**. That is a hybrid, and our graph already has the hook for it.

## 1. Why Gemini and LLM-encoder models count limbs better than SDXL

- **The text encoder matters more than the image model.** Imagen found that "increasing the size of the language model in Imagen boosts both sample fidelity and image-text alignment much more than increasing the size of the image diffusion model" (frozen T5-XXL). [arXiv 2205.11487](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11487)
- **CLIP text embeddings act like bags of words.** Contrastive VLMs largely ignore word order and relations, because retrieval-style training never forces them to learn composition. [Yuksekgonul et al., arXiv 2210.01936](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01936). SDXL conditions only on CLIP ViT-L + OpenCLIP bigG with a 77-token context. So "two eye stalks on top, four legs per side, chelae" reaches the UNet as a soft mix of concepts, not as constraints.
- **SD3 measured T5's contribution.** Dropping T5 barely changes aesthetics (50% win rate) and only slightly changes general adherence (46%). The large gains appear "for complex prompts involving either highly detailed descriptions of a scene or larger amounts of written text". [SD3 paper, arXiv 2403.03206](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.03206). Our anatomy prompts are exactly the detailed-description case.
- **Counting benchmark (GenEval counting / overall), from the Qwen-Image report:** SD3 Medium 0.63/0.62, SD3.5 Large 0.73/0.71, FLUX.1-dev 0.74/0.66, HiDream-I1 0.79/0.83, GPT Image 1 0.85/0.84, Qwen-Image 0.89/0.87. DPG-Bench: **SDXL 74.65**, FLUX.1-dev 83.84, HiDream 85.89, Qwen-Image 88.32. [Qwen-Image tech report, arXiv 2508.02324](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.02324). Qwen-Image's text side is Qwen2.5-VL-7B (same source). The SDXL GenEval counting number is not in that table: unverified here.
- **Gemini / Nano Banana:** Google publishes no architecture detail for its image models beyond "native" image generation inside Gemini. Any explanation from LLM world knowledge (the model "knows" crab anatomy as text and images jointly) is plausible but **unverified**.
- **Hypothesis (unverified):** SDXL's "face on the shell front" is a training-data prior. Cartoon and mascot crabs in LAION-style data commonly have eyes on the carapace, and CLIP cannot tell "eye stalks" apart from "eyes". IP-Adapter from our bright legacy crab references may reinforce this.

## 2. Local models that fit 12 GB / ~26 GB disk

### 2a. Z-Image-Turbo (Tongyi-MAI): recommended first local try
- 6B parameters, Apache-2.0, 8 NFEs at guidance 0, "fits comfortably within 16G VRAM consumer devices". [HF card](https://huggingface.co/Tongyi-MAI/Z-Image-Turbo)
- ComfyUI files (native workflow template): `z_image_turbo_bf16` 12.3 GB, **`z_image_turbo_int8_convrot` 6.2 GB**, `nvfp4` 4.51 GB (Blackwell only, so not for our 4070). Text encoder `qwen_3_4b` 8.04 GB, **`fp8_mixed` 5.63 GB**, fp4 3.48 GB. VAE `ae.safetensors`. [Comfy-Org repo, diffusion_models](https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/z_image_turbo/tree/main/split_files/diffusion_models), [text_encoders](https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/z_image_turbo/tree/main/split_files/text_encoders), [ComfyUI tutorial](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/image/z-image/z-image-turbo). The VAE is listed as `ae.safetensors`, i.e. the Flux VAE (~335 MB, size unverified for this repo).
- Anatomy evidence: vendor Elo rankings only, no GenEval in the card. **Unverified for arthropods.** That is why it is an experiment, not a decision.
- Nunchaku supports Z-Image-Turbo. [nunchaku README](https://github.com/nunchaku-tech/nunchaku)
- ControlNet: `alibaba-pai/Z-Image-Turbo-Fun-Controlnet-Union` covers Canny, HED, Depth, Pose and MLSD, Apache-2.0, recommended scale 0.65-0.80. The card shows VideoX-Fun inference only, so ComfyUI support is unverified. [HF](https://huggingface.co/alibaba-pai/Z-Image-Turbo-Fun-Controlnet-Union)
- IP-Adapter / style reference: none found. Transparent generation: none found. Use our `matte` step (ISNet) or BiRefNet instead.
- Pixel LoRAs exist, e.g. [elusarca pixel-art-style Z-Image Turbo](https://huggingface.co/reverentelusarca/elusarca-pixel-art-style-lora-zimage-turbo) (licence not checked). SDXL LoRAs do not load on Z-Image. [lilting.ch](https://lilting.ch/en/articles/z-image-pixel-art-conversion)

[Comfy-Org flux2-klein-4B](https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/flux2-klein-4B/tree/main/split_files/diffusion_models), [vae](https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/flux2-klein-4B/tree/main/split_files/vae). BFL FP8 checkpoints cut VRAM by up to 40% (search summary of BFL card; exact file size unverified).
- Why it matters for us: multi-reference input can carry **style from our approved sprites** while the LLM-conditioned model handles anatomy. That covers the job IP-Adapter does in SDXL.
- Pixel LoRAs: [svntax-dev pixel spritesheet LoRA](https://huggingface.co/svntax-dev/pixel_spritesheet_4walk_small_lora_v1/blob/main/README.md) recommends the **base** 4B (the distilled model degrades pixel quality), 512² generation, and a 4x k-centroid downscale. - Pixel LoRAs: none found (unverified).

## 3. Hybrid: strong structure, SDXL style

- **xinsir union ProMax control types:** Openpose, Depth, Canny, Lineart, AnimeLineart, MLSD, Scribble, HED, PiDi (softedge), TEED, Segment, Normal, plus Tile/Inpaint/Outpaint. [HF](https://huggingface.co/xinsir/controlnet-union-sdxl-1.0). Our graph already defines `LINEART_TYPE = 'canny/lineart/anime_lineart/mlsd'` and loads the control image from `control.base64` (`scripts/asset-gen/lib/workflow.mjs`). Swapping the procedural scribble for a real image is a CLI/batch-field change, not a graph change.
- **Preprocessors in our installed comfyui_controlnet_aux:** Canny, HED, Standard/Realistic/Anime/Manga Lineart, PiDiNet, TEED, Scribble, Binary; depth MiDaS/Zoe/Depth Anything V2; normals. [README](https://github.com/Fannovel16/comfyui_controlnet_aux)
- **Which signal to use.** For a strong-model output (clean, flat, already sprite-like), run AnimeLineart or Canny at strength 0.6-0.8 until ~0.6-0.8 of the steps. This carries exact leg and joint placement while SDXL/IP-Adapter repaint the style. For a **photo** of a crab, Depth Anything V2 depth or a soft edge (HED/PiDi/TEED) is more forgiving than canny on shell texture (expectation, unverified). Strengths are starting points, not sourced.
- **Alternative: img2img.** Encode the strong-model image as the initial latent at denoise ~0.5-0.65 with IP-Adapter. Silhouette and counts survive, colours and pixel style get replaced (standard SDXL behaviour; values unverified). LayerDiffuse still works here because SDXL does the final sampling. That is the main reason to keep SDXL as the last stage.
- **The pose must match the sprite view.** Our sprites use a front (`crabFront`) or top (`crab`) view, so the reference must be the same view.

## 4. Gemini image API as a backend

- **Model ids now:** `gemini-nano-banana-2.1`, `gemini-3.1-flash-image` (Nano Banana 2), `gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image` (2 Lite), `gemini-3-pro-image` (Pro), `gemini-2.5-flash-image` (legacy). [Image generation docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation). The pricing page says `gemini-2.5-flash-image` "will shut down on October 2, 2026", so the exact model the user tried is gone and the successors must be re-tested. [Pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing)
- **Price per output image (standard / batch):** Nano Banana 2.1 1K $0.0336 / $0.0168, 2K $0.0504. Nano Banana 2 0.5K $0.045, 1K $0.067. 2 Lite 1K $0.0336. Pro 1K/2K $0.134. **No free tier** for image output. [Pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing). A 50-enemy x 4-candidate run is ≈ $7 at 2.1 1K standard.
- **Rate limits:** IPM applies to image models, with values per tier shown in AI Studio. Not published on the page. [Rate limits](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits)
- **Resolution:** 1K/2K/4K, plus 0.5K on 3.1 Flash. Many aspect ratios. [Docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation)
- **Reference images:** "up to 14 reference images". Per-model limits differ (e.g. 3 style references for Pro); the docs table is garbled, so the exact mapping is unverified. Same page. We can send our approved sprites as style references.
- **Transparency:** not mentioned in the docs, so assume opaque. Our LayerDiffuse cannot apply: it is SDXL/SD1.5 only, and its "Extract FG from Blended + BG" mode needs the known background. [ComfyUI-layerdiffuse](https://github.com/huchenlei/ComfyUI-layerdiffuse). Prompt for a flat contrasting background and run our ISNet `matte`, or use [BiRefNet](https://github.com/ZhengPeng7/BiRefNet) (MIT, HR-matting variants, official Comfy-Org ComfyUI integration). Or use Gemini only for structure and let SDXL + LayerDiffuse redraw it (Section 3).
- **SynthID:** "All generated images include a SynthID watermark." [Docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation). It is invisible, and our pixelate/quantize step will likely degrade it (unverified). That changes nothing legally.
- **ComfyUI:** a native "Google Gemini Image" partner/API node exists. [ComfyUI blog](https://blog.comfy.org/p/nano-banana-via-comfyui-api-nodes). It needs a Comfy account and credits. Calling REST directly from Node (`generateContent` with `responseModalities: IMAGE`) is simpler for us.
- **Terms:** "Google won't claim ownership over that content". On paid services "Google doesn't use your prompts… to improve our products"; unpaid content is used for that. You must be 18+. In the EEA/UK/CH only Paid Services may be used. No clause found barring commercial use of outputs; there is a ban on building competing models. [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms). The consumer Gemini app the user tried has separate terms (not checked; unverified).

## 5. Other arthropod-specific levers (sourced only)

- **Generate large, then reduce to the true grid.** Generating at sprite size fails, so render large and downscale. [aigaming.nuilab.org sprites](https://aigaming.nuilab.org/workflows/sprites/). The klein pixel LoRA author recommends 512² and a 4x k-centroid downscale. [svntax-dev](https://huggingface.co/svntax-dev/pixel_spritesheet_4walk_small_lora_v1/blob/main/README.md). We already render at SDXL-native size and pixelate. A k-centroid downscale instead of our current reducer is worth an A/B.
- **Inpaint a bad leg instead of rerolling:** xinsir ProMax includes inpainting. [HF](https://huggingface.co/xinsir/controlnet-union-sdxl-1.0). Our inpaint-nodes are installed. Useful for fixing one merged leg on an otherwise good candidate.
- **SD3.5 Skip Layer Guidance** for anatomy coherency applies only if we adopt SD3.5. [HF](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-diffusion-3.5-medium)
- **Not found / unverified:** sourced evidence that "front view" / "top-down" wording or negative prompts ("face on shell") fix arthropod anatomy in SDXL. Regional prompting for limbs: no primary source found.

## What to try first (≤ 26 GB disk)

1. **E1: real-reference ControlNet, 0 GB, about 1 hour.** Add `--control-image <path>` (batch field `controlImage`) that loads a PNG as `control.base64`. Run it through a comfyui_controlnet_aux preprocessor node (`AnimeLineArtPreprocessor`, `CannyEdgePreprocessor` or `DepthAnythingV2Preprocessor`) placed before `controlApply`, with `type: LINEART_TYPE` (or `'depth'`). Inputs: (a) a CC0 front-view crab photo, (b) a Gemini/Z-Image crab from E2/E3. Sweep strength 0.5/0.7/0.9 × end 0.6/0.8. Compare against the `crabFront` scribble on the same seeds.
2. **E2: Z-Image-Turbo structure, ~12.2 GB.** Download `z_image_turbo_int8_convrot.safetensors` (6.2 GB) to `models/diffusion_models`, `qwen_3_4b_fp8_mixed.safetensors` (5.63 GB) to `models/text_encoders`, and `ae.safetensors` to `models/vae`. Load the ComfyUI Z-Image template first and confirm it runs in Krita's ComfyUI (check `/object_info` for the loaders). Prompt: "pixel art sprite of a crab, front view, domed carapace, two eye stalks on top, two large claws, four jointed walking legs on each side, plain flat grey background". 8 steps, cfg 1, 1024². Count legs and eyes on 8 seeds per species (crab, spider, scorpion, beetle). If good, add `buildZImageGraph()` next to `buildGraph()` in `lib/workflow.mjs` and use it as the E1 control source ("two-pass" mode), or run it standalone through `matte → trim → pixelate → outline → quantize`.
3. **E3: Gemini baseline, $≈1.** Run 4 species × 4 images on `gemini-nano-banana-2.1` at 1K, with 3 of our approved sprites attached as style references. This gives a quality ceiling and a structure source for E1. Use a paid key (EEA). Do not commit outputs until the art review.
4. **E4: FLUX.2 klein 4B, +8.1 GB (total ≈ 20.3 GB with E2).** Download `flux-2-klein-base-4b.safetensors` (7.75 GB) and `flux2-vae.safetensors` (336 MB); the text encoder is shared with E2. Test multi-reference: 3 approved sprites + "a crab in the same pixel-art style, front view…". If style and anatomy both hold, this path could skip SDXL except for alpha. Watch VRAM (~13 GB claimed) and enable offload if it OOMs.
5. **Defer:** Qwen-Image (disk), FLUX.1 dev/Kontext (licence), HiDream (disk), SD3.5 (weaker counting).

Acceptance metric for all of these: count-correct rate (claws 2, eye stalks 2 on top, walking legs 4+4 for crabs, 8 legs for spiders, 6 for insects, pincers + segmented tail for scorpions) across 8 seeds, judged on the final post-processed sprite, plus a style match against the approved references.
